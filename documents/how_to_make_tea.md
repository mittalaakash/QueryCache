Preparation
Boil fresh water: Pour fresh, cold, filtered water into a kettle or pan and bring it to a rolling boil.Measure the tea: Place one tea bag or one teaspoon of loose-leaf tea into your cup or teapot for every single cup you plan to make

Action
Pour and steep: Pour the hot boiling water directly over the tea leaves or bag. Let it steep for 3 to 5 minutes so the full flavor develops.Remove or strain: Take out the tea bag or use a small mesh strainer to pour the liquid, leaving the loose leaves behind

Finishing
Add extras and serve: Stir in sugar, honey, or a splash of milk to taste, then enjoy your hot tea.
